# Business Continuity and Crisis Management Plan

Each critical business process must maintain a business impact analysis, dependency map, recovery strategy, emergency contacts, and alternate operating procedure. Critical manufacturing plants and distribution centers must exercise their continuity plans annually. Corporate crisis-management exercises occur twice per year.

A crisis is declared when an event threatens life safety, causes or is expected to cause a Tier 1 service outage longer than two hours, stops a critical production line for more than four hours, creates a material regulatory exposure, or significantly disrupts multiple regions. The Crisis Management Team is led by the Chief Operating Officer or delegate.

Business units must account for personnel, protect life safety, stabilize operations, communicate verified information, and record decisions. External statements are coordinated through Communications and Legal. Lessons learned and corrective actions are documented within ten business days after deactivation.
