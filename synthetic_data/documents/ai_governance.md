# Responsible AI Governance Standard

Every production AI use case must have a named business owner, technical owner, documented purpose, approved data sources, intended users, human oversight design, risk classification, test evidence, and monitoring plan. AI systems are classified as Low, Moderate, High, or Prohibited risk.

High-risk use cases include decisions affecting employment, credit, safety, regulated eligibility, significant customer rights, or autonomous control of physical equipment. High-risk systems require review by Legal, Security, Privacy, Model Risk, and the Responsible AI Council before production release. A human must be able to review, override, or stop consequential decisions.

Production generative AI must protect confidential information, resist prompt injection, identify retrieved sources, record model and prompt versions, monitor quality and harmful output, and provide a fallback when confidence is insufficient. Models may not be trained on customer data unless the contract and approved privacy notice permit it.

Material model changes, new data sources, or a change in intended use require revalidation. High-risk models are reviewed at least quarterly; Moderate-risk models at least annually. Prohibited uses include unlawful discrimination, covert manipulation, and fully autonomous lethal decision-making.
