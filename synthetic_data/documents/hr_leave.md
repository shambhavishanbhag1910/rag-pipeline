# United States Paid Time Off and Leave Guide

Regular full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per calendar year, accrued each pay period. Up to five unused PTO days may be carried into the next calendar year; amounts above five days expire unless local law requires otherwise. Managers should normally receive at least two weeks of notice for planned absences longer than three consecutive workdays.

Employees receive ten paid company holidays. Eligible employees may take up to five paid bereavement days for the death of an immediate family member and up to three days for an extended family member. Jury duty is paid for up to ten working days, after which additional time is unpaid unless required by law.

Requests for medical, parental, military, disability, or family-care leave must be directed to the Leave Administration team because eligibility and documentation depend on applicable law and plan rules. Employees should not send medical details to their direct manager.
