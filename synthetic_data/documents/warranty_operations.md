# Vehicle Warranty Operations Playbook

## Claim submission
Dealers must submit standard warranty claims within 30 calendar days after repair completion. The claim must include the customer complaint, technician cause or finding, correction performed, labor operations, replaced parts, fault codes when available, repair date, mileage, and supporting invoice. Claims with missing complaint-cause-correction information are returned for correction.

## Review and payment
The automated rules engine checks eligibility, coverage, duplicate claims, labor time, part pricing, campaign applicability, and required attachments. Low-risk clean claims may be auto-approved. Safety, emissions, repeat-repair, high-cost, or suspected abuse claims require analyst review. Approved claims are targeted for payment within ten business days.

## Supplier recovery
When a failed component is supplier-responsible, the recovery packet must include the accepted dealer claim, part and supplier identifiers, failure evidence, causal analysis, population and exposure, contractual recovery basis, and financial calculation. Recovery cases above $100,000 require Warranty Director approval before debit issuance.

## Field action trigger
A cross-functional investigation must be opened when the same safety-related failure appears on three vehicles, when a repeat repair rate exceeds five percent, or when projected twelve-month warranty exposure exceeds $500,000. Engineering confirms root cause; the platform must not auto-confirm root cause without accountable human approval.
