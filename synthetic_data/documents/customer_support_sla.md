# Enterprise Customer Support Service Levels

Priority 1 means the production service is unavailable or a critical business process has stopped with no viable workaround. Support acknowledges Priority 1 cases within 15 minutes, engages technical response within 30 minutes, and provides updates every hour. The restoration target is four hours, but it is a target rather than a contractual guarantee unless the customer agreement says otherwise.

Priority 2 means severe degradation with a workaround or major functionality unavailable. Acknowledgement is within one hour and updates are provided every four hours during the customer's business day. Priority 3 acknowledgement is within eight business hours. Priority 4 requests are acknowledged within two business days.

Customers must provide impact, affected users, timestamps, examples, recent changes, and available logs. Support may lower priority when the stated impact does not match the priority definition, but it must explain the change to the customer.
