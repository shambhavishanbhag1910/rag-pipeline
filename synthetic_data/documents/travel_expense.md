# Business Travel and Expense Standard

Travelers must book through the approved travel platform. Economy class is required for flights under six hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights of six hours or more with manager approval. Business class requires an executive vice president's written approval and is limited to scheduled flight time of ten hours or more, medical accommodation, or exceptional business need.

Hotels should remain within the city rate cap shown in the travel platform. When the conference hotel exceeds the cap, it may be used if the total trip cost is lower or a vice president approves the exception. Meals are reimbursed up to $75 per day domestically and $100 per day internationally, including tips. Alcohol is not reimbursable except for a pre-approved customer event.

Expense reports must be submitted within 15 calendar days after the trip. Itemized receipts are required for expenses of $25 or more. Personal expenses, traffic fines, minibar charges, and commuting between home and the normal work location are not reimbursable.
