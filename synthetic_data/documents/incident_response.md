# Cybersecurity Incident Response Standard

## Severity definitions
A Severity 1 incident includes confirmed ransomware, material production outage caused by a cyber event, active compromise of privileged credentials, or unauthorized disclosure of Restricted data. Severity 2 covers contained malware, suspected compromise with credible evidence, or a security event affecting a non-critical business service.

## Response targets
The Security Operations Center must acknowledge a Severity 1 incident within 15 minutes and establish an incident command channel within 30 minutes. The incident commander must provide executive updates at least every 60 minutes until containment. A Severity 2 incident must be acknowledged within one hour and receive an initial containment plan within four hours.

## Required workflow
The response team must preserve evidence, record a timeline, identify affected identities and assets, contain the threat, eradicate persistence, recover services, and validate monitoring. Legal and Privacy must be engaged immediately when personal data, contractual notification duties, or law-enforcement contact may be involved. Communications to customers, regulators, media, or suppliers may be issued only by authorized Communications and Legal representatives.

## Post-incident review
A blameless post-incident review is required within five business days for Severity 1 and within ten business days for Severity 2. Corrective actions must have owners and due dates. High-risk actions are tracked weekly by the Cyber Risk Committee until closure.
