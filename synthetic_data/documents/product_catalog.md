# Acme Industrial Analytics Product Catalog

FactoryPulse Standard provides machine connectivity, hourly production dashboards, downtime categorization, and 13 months of online history. FactoryPulse Enterprise adds predictive maintenance, cross-plant benchmarking, role-based workflow, unlimited online history, and private networking. Enterprise customers receive a 99.9 percent availability service objective.

WarrantyIQ Standard provides claim intake, validation rules, duplicate detection, coverage checks, dashboards, and dealer feedback. WarrantyIQ Enterprise adds failure-pattern detection, engineering investigation packets, supplier recovery workflow, field-action scenario analysis, and governed AI assistance. WarrantyIQ does not autonomously confirm root cause or issue supplier debits.

SupplierShield provides supplier onboarding, financial and cyber risk monitoring, capacity alerts, scorecards, and corrective-action tracking. The Premium edition adds multi-tier dependency mapping and geopolitical disruption scenarios.
