# Enterprise Data Retention and Disposal Schedule

Business records must be retained according to legal, regulatory, contractual, and operational requirements. A legal hold overrides every normal deletion schedule until Legal releases the hold in writing.

Financial ledgers, tax records, and external audit evidence are retained for seven years after the end of the relevant fiscal year. Executed customer and supplier contracts are retained for seven years after expiration or termination. Product design history files, safety validation evidence, and regulated quality records are retained for the product life plus fifteen years.

General collaboration messages are retained for three years. Routine system logs are retained online for 90 days and in lower-cost archival storage for one year. Security logs for identity, privileged access, network security, and production cloud activity are retained for thirteen months. Applicant records are retained for two years unless local law requires longer.

At the end of the retention period, records must be deleted securely and irreversibly. Disposal jobs must be logged. Backups follow their own rotation and are not used to extend normal retention intentionally.
